# unco product research — "Warm & Easy Home"

Researched 2026-10-27 with the CJdropshipping (CJ) connector, read-only. Nothing was ordered, added to a cart or paid.

## The theme

**unco makes home warmer and easier.** It sells practical, good-looking things for people who plan to stay in their own homes, comfortably, for a long time. That's "Everyday life, uncomplicated" applied to the home.

Why this theme:
- **The "cozy home" look leads fall 2026.** Coverage points to warm, earthy palettes (caramel, rust, olive, cream), soft 2700K lighting and heavier textiles. Target, IKEA and Pottery Barn are reported to have built their fall merchandising around it. ([Homes & Gardens](https://www.homesandgardens.com/decor/fall-trends-2026), [Builder](https://www.builderonline.com/design/cozy-is-in-the-home-design-trends-defining-fall/), [NewsBreak](https://www.newsbreak.com/her-life-adventures-383428129/4896365614679-cozy-home-is-the-new-trend-for-fall-2026-here-s-how-to-get-the-look-on-any-budget))
- **Aging in place is growing.** People over 65 are now reported to be the largest group of home-remodeling spenders, and more than 11,200 Americans turn 65 every day. ([William Blair](https://www.williamblair.com/-/media/downloads/eqr/2025/williamblair-demographics-as-commerce-aging-in-place-opportunity.pdf), [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/baby-boomers-remodeling-to-age-in-place-will-be-the-trend-harvard-study/))
- **How to reach them on Facebook.** This audience responds to ads about home, health, family and hobbies, and carousel ads that show the products do well. ([Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/marketing-baby-boomers))

Rules for picking products:
1. The item makes daily life at home warmer, safer or easier, and you can tell from one photo.
2. It looks good. Nothing that looks clinical: no "for the elderly" wording, and no medical claims.
3. It ships from the US. The priority is CJ's US-supplier listings with delivery built into the price: 1–3 days, $0 extra shipping.
4. It retails at $40 or more, or can be sold in a bundle that does, so the roughly $10 ad cost per sale still leaves a 25%+ margin.

I couldn't see Facebook's own trend data. Before spending on ads, search each hero product in Meta's Ad Library; ads that have run for months are a good sign the product sells.

- Cat-shaped gravity feeder (too cute for the brand)
