## Site ideas to make fall pop

1. **"Lamplight" evening mode.** After 6 pm on the visitor's own clock, the cream background warms slightly and product photos get a soft warm glow, like a lamp turning on. It's subtle, it happens automatically, and most stores don't do anything like it.
2. **A few falling leaves, once.** On the first page load of a visit, three to five illustrated leaves drift down across the hero section in about 4 seconds, then stop. It doesn't repeat on later pages, and it's switched off for visitors whose device is set to reduce motion.
3. **"Stitched" header edge.** A thin caramel line in a stitched pattern along the bottom of the purple header, like a seam on a wool blanket. The purple header itself doesn't change.
4. **Leaf section dividers.** A small hand-drawn leaf between homepage sections, in place of plain horizontal rules.
5. **"Make the evenings easier" homepage story.** Instead of a product grid, the hero tells one short story about an evening at home, with each product shown where it's used: the fireplace in the living room, the shawl in an armchair, the motion lights in the hallway.
6. **Hover glow on product cards.** Hovering (or tapping, on phones) gives a card a warm caramel outline and a slight lift, like a lit window.
7. **"First frost" banner.** When the first frost is forecast in the visitor's area, the homepage banner reads "First frost is on its way: get the heated wrap delivered in 1–3 days." This needs a weather lookup, so it's a later nice-to-have.

## Ad angle

"Evenings are getting longer. Here's how to make them warmer." Use carousel ads that show the fireplace, the shawl and the motion lights in one warm-lit living room.
