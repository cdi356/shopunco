# Spring — "Easy Garden" (Mar 1 – May 31)

**The pitch:** gardening that's kinder to your knees and back.

Gardening is one of the biggest hobbies for people 50 and older, and spring is when they spend on it. The angle is not "gardening gear" in general: it's garden gear that means less bending, kneeling, lifting and watering.

## Site ideas to make spring pop

1. **A garden that grows through the season.** A small illustrated garden along the bottom edge of the purple header starts as bare soil on March 1, sprouts in April and is in full bloom (with lavender, matching the purple) by May. Returning visitors watch it grow, and it's tied to the calendar, not animated.
2. **Seed-packet product cards.** Product cards styled like vintage seed packets: an illustration at the top, the name in a serif font, and practical details on the back, revealed when hovered or tapped.
3. **"What to plant this week."** A small homepage box with one seasonal tip based on the visitor's region, using their ZIP code if they're signed in, or a simple region picker. It gives people a reason to come back weekly and to open emails.
4. **A few drifting petals.** Same rule as the fall leaves: a few petals, once per visit, off for reduced motion.
5. **Customer garden photos.** Shoppers submit a photo of their raised bed or bird bath in use to appear in a "From our customers' gardens" section. This audience trusts real photos from people like them more than ads.

## Ad angle

"Gardening without the sore knees." Use a short video: kneel on the kneeler, flip it, sit on it.
