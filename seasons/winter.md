# Winter — "Rest & Reset" (Jan 1 – Feb 28)

**The pitch:** a calmer, tidier, more comfortable home for the quiet months.

After the holidays, people want to get organized, rest, and make the house work better. This is the time for "for yourself" purchases, the opposite of holiday gifting.

## Site ideas to make winter pop

1. **Frost on the edges.** A faint ice-crystal pattern in the corners of the page background, a little stronger in the morning (visitor's clock) and fading by noon. It's purely decorative, and text sits on plain frost.
2. **"Reset your home" room picker.** An illustrated floor plan (kitchen, bedroom, entry, living room). Tap a room to see a short checklist and the products that help, such as "Entry: handrail, motion lights, boot tray".
3. **Printable new-year checklist.** A one-page "Easy home checklist" PDF that customers get by signing up for the email list. It grows the list and is genuinely useful.
4. **Quiet mode.** The site already feels calm in winter: fewer banners, more white space, bigger product photos. Less on the page is the special effect.
5. **"Before and after" sliders.** A drag slider comparing a cluttered cabinet with the same cabinet after using unco organizers. This works very well on Facebook too.

## Ad angle

"A calmer home for the new year." Use before-and-after photos of a closet or cabinet.
