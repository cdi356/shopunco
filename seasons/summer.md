## Site ideas to make summer pop

1. **Sky that follows the clock.** The thin band under the purple header shows the visitor's time of day: a pale blue morning, bright midday, a golden-coral sunset in the evening and a deep blue sky with a few stars at night. It's quiet, unique, and makes the site feel alive.
2. **Fireflies at dusk.** In the evening (visitor's clock), a few soft glowing dots drift in the hero section for a few seconds. They're off for visitors whose device is set to reduce motion.
3. **"Grandkids are coming" checklist.** An interactive checklist (guest bed, night lights, step railing, something to do outside), with each item linked to a product. It answers a real worry and sells the bundle.
4. **Lemonade-stand sale banner.** For the July 4th and Labor Day sales: a hand-drawn stand sign that reads "Summer sale: 15% off porch favorites".
5. **Postcard product cards.** On the summer collection page, product cards look like vintage postcards, with a stamp in the corner that reads "Ships from the US in 1–3 days."

## Ad angle

"Make the porch the best room in the house." Use a golden-hour photo of a porch with the cushions, a lantern and a side table.
