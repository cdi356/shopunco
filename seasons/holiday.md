# Holiday — "Give Easy" (Nov 21 – Dec 31)

**The pitch:** thoughtful, useful gifts for the people you love, delivered from US warehouses in time for the holidays.

This is the season when people in their 50s and 60s buy for spouses, siblings, their own parents and grandkids, and are often shopping for themselves at the same time. US-warehouse delivery is the big advantage here: when shoppers worry about gifts arriving in time, "arrives in 1–3 days" is the selling point.

## Site ideas to make the holidays pop

1. **"Order by" countdown.** A banner that reads "Order by Dec 18 to arrive by Christmas" and counts down the days. Products shipping from China get an earlier cutoff, shown on their own page. This one feature reassures this audience more than any other.
2. **Gift finder.** Three big buttons and three steps: who it's for (partner, parent, friend, grandkid, a pet), what they enjoy (staying warm, gardening, reading, their pet), and a budget. The result is a short list of three gifts. It's easy to tap on a phone.
3. **"Send it as a gift" at checkout.** A checkbox to ship the order to someone else, add a gift note, and leave prices off the packing slip.
4. **Twinkling string lights along the header.** A row of small gold lights hangs below the purple header, twinkling slowly; they stay still for visitors whose device is set to reduce motion. The purple header itself doesn't change.
5. **"12 days of easy."** Dec 1–12: each day reveals one featured product with a small discount, like an advent calendar. Returning visitors have something new to open each day, and it gives Facebook a fresh daily post.
6. **Snow on the hero only.** A light snowfall over the top homepage image, never over products or text, and stopped when the visitor scrolls.
7. **Gift-tag product cards.** Products in the holiday collection get a small gold tag in the corner that says "Great gift".

## Ad angle

"The gift they'll actually use." Show one person opening a heated shawl, and mention arrival in 1–3 days.
